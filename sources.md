# Go-to sources

## Company financials
- UK: Companies House, `https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/<number>/filing-history`. Shows the accounts type and date, officers, charges and share allotments. Aggregators such as Endole and comptrack are often wrong, so label their figures unverified.
- US: SEC EDGAR 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K filings.
- Elsewhere: the national company registry.
- Funding: Crunchbase, CB Insights, and the company's press page.

## News (last 6 months)
- The company newsroom (`/news`, `/press`).
- WebSearch: `<company> <year> partnership OR launch OR funding`, `<company> appoints OR hires chief`.
- Trade press for the sector. For EVs, for example: electrive, EV Fleet World, Transport+Energy, BusinessGreen.

## AI / stack
- The company's own job ads (data, ML and engineering) are the best signal for the stack.
- Engineering blog, GitHub org, conference talks (YouTube) and arXiv.

## Interviewer
- LinkedIn. When the www URL redirects, try the country subdomain, e.g. `uk.linkedin.com/in/<handle>`. Their posts reveal their function.

## Salary
- UK: ITJobsWatch, `itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/<city>/<role>.do`, gives the median and percentiles.
- Recruiter ads for comparable roles.
- Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Welcome to the Jungle (Otta).
